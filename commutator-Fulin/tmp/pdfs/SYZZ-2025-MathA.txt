Optimal distribution estimates for commutators and
Marcinkiewicz multipliers

Abstract
The main objective of this paper is to investigate the distributional estimates for (i)
commutators with Calderón–Zygmund integral operators; (ii) Marcinkiewicz multi-
pliers; (iii) Littlewood–Paley square function, via semigroup {C α }α>0 generated by
Cesàro operator. In each of the cases (i)–(iii) we obtain new estimates of the distribution
of elements in the range of the underlying operators in terms of the distribution func-
tion of the input function. Our method allows us to obtain optimal estimates shedding
additional light at the results due to Pérez (J Funct Anal 128(1):163–185, 1995), Tao
and Wright (Rev Mat Iberoam 17(3):521–558, 2001), Bakas et al. (Rev Mat Iberoam
2024), Bourgain (On the Behavior of the Constant in the Littlewood–Paley Inequal-
ity, Geometric Aspects of Functional Analysis (1987–88), Lecture Notes in Math.,
vol. 1376, pp. 202–208. Springer, Berlin, 1989). The main feature of the distributional
form inequalities lies in its broad applicability across diverse problems in analysis, e.g.
they allow obtaining estimates in wide range of symmetric quasi-Banach interpolation
spaces between L p and L q (1 < p < q < ∞), not just for L p -spaces (1 < p < ∞).

1.2 Technical innovation

Our main technical innovation underlying the proofs of Theorems 1.2, 1.4 and 1.5 is
the identifying of the following notion, which serves as a bridge between Theorems A,
B, and C.
Definition 1.7 Let ( , F, ν) be a measure space. We say that a linear operator Z
defined on the space L 0 ( , F, ν) of all measurable functions on ( , F, ν) sat-
isfies the weak Cesàro property with parameter α > 0 (denoted by WCP(α)) if
for every f ∈ L 0 ( , F, ν) supported on a set of finite measure and for every
λ ≥ C α μ( f )(ν(supp( f ))), we have

       λν ({x ∈     : |Z f |(x) > λ}) ≤ ν(supp( f )) · C α μ( f )(ν(supp( f ))).       (1.5)

   For α = 1, the operator Z has the property WCP(1) iff it is of weak type (1, 1). Since
Calderón–Zygmund integral operator T is of weak type (1, 1), it follows that T satisfies
WCP(1). In Theorem 5.1 below, we show that the commutator [Mφ , T ] possesses
WCP(2). In Proposition 6.6, we prove that each Marcinkiewicz multiplier Tm satisfies
WCP( 23 ). The discovery of weak Cesàro property led us to an abstract extrapolation
result, i.e. Theorem 3.1, which is the crucial tool in the proofs of Theorems 1.2 and 1.4.
In Lemma 5.7, we demonstrate that weak Cesàro property is the right tool of endpoint
estimates and subsequent applications in the interpolation theory.
